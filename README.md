# UNAVAILABLE Installation

Create a one-page cinematic launch website for the luxury minimal

streetwear brand UNAVAILABLE, LATELY.

The website must feel like an architectural fashion installation rather

than a standard ecommerce template.

VISUAL ENVIRONMENT

Create a dark brutalist concrete interior with large architectural slabs,

deep shadows, soft directional daylight, subtle material texture and

significant negative space.

The environment must feel photorealistic, premium, quiet and cinematic.

Do not create a generic digital 3D background, neon environment, gaming

interface or futuristic technology scene.

Use the uploaded architectural concrete image as the exact environmental,

lighting, texture and composition reference.

PRODUCT ACCURACY

Use only the uploaded transparent product images for the five heavyweight

t-shirts:

1. Deep Charcoal Black

2. Washed Graphite

3. Coastal Sand

4. Bone White

5. Midnight Navy

Do not generate replacement shirts.

Do not use stock products.

Do not create generic 3D t-shirt models.

Do not recolor one shirt to imitate the separate colorways.

Preserve the exact silhouette, collar, sleeves, proportions, fabric

texture and internal neck label shown in the uploaded product assets.

HERO

Create a full-screen 16:9 hero.

Place the five shirt images inside the concrete environment at different

visual depths, creating the illusion of a three-dimensional installation.

The composition should feel controlled and editorial, not like a product

grid.

Headline:

NOT MADE

TO BE AVAILABLE.

Supporting text:

Five elevated heavyweight tees.

Produced in limited quantities.

Primary button:

VIEW THE COLLECTION

Secondary text link:

RELEASE NOTIFICATION

MOTION

Use restrained premium motion.

Add slow camera movement into the concrete environment.

Use subtle parallax between the foreground concrete, product images and

background architecture.

Add extremely slow floating movement to the shirts.

Allow cursor movement to shift the lighting and depth slightly.

During scroll, separate the five shirts gradually into individual

colorway presentations.

Do not spin the shirts.

Do not create fast transitions.

Do not use bouncy easing.

Do not animate every text element.

Do not create distracting 3D effects.

PRODUCT STORY

Add a minimal section with the headline:

FIVE COLORS.

ONE CONSTRUCTION.

Reveal the following lines one at a time during scroll:

HEAVYWEIGHT DOUBLE-KNIT

STRUCTURED COLLAR

TAILORED DRAPE

NO EXTERIOR BRANDING

COLORWAY ARCHIVE

Create an interactive full-screen product section.

Show one uploaded shirt image at large scale.

Add a minimal vertical selector:

01 DEEP CHARCOAL

02 WASHED GRAPHITE

03 COASTAL SAND

04 BONE WHITE

05 MIDNIGHT NAVY

When a colorway is selected, replace the product with the corresponding

uploaded product image and make a subtle adjustment to the surrounding

environmental light.

PRODUCT DETAILS

Create close product-detail sections showing:

elevated collar

fabric texture

reinforced shoulder seam

internal woven label

heavyweight hem construction

Use the headline:

BUILT TO HOLD ITS FORM.

Add the text:

Dense double-knit construction gives the garment a structured silhouette

without sacrificing comfort.

Add another section:

NOTHING EXTERNAL.

No chest graphic.

No sleeve mark.

No visible logo.

Only the garment remains.

COUNTDOWN

Create a full-screen release countdown section.

Text:

RELEASE 001

00 DAYS : 00 HOURS : 00 MINUTES : 00 SECONDS

Button:

NOTIFY ME

Supporting line:

Limited quantities. No planned restock.

FINAL FRAME

Fade the concrete environment gradually into darkness.

Center the brand name:

UNAVAILABLE, LATELY.

Below it place:

ST. PETERSBURG, FLORIDA

27.7731° N, 82.6400° W

DESIGN RULES

Use black, charcoal concrete, warm grey and off-white only.

Use a premium grotesk sans-serif typeface.

Keep the layout minimal and highly structured.

Use thin borders and small editorial labels.

Maintain large areas of negative space.

Make all sections responsive.

Do not use rounded cards.

Do not use glowing effects.

Do not use glassmorphism.

Do not use gradient buttons.

Do not use stock photography.

Do not create visible website mockup frames.

Do not add generic product descriptions, testimonials, fake reviews or

fake statistics.
